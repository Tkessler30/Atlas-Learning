DEEP DIVE

Choose a subject and go deeper.

Deep Dive is the user-facing version of your adaptive frontier: Atlas finds the next useful concept inside a subject, then lets you learn it, stretch it, or apply it.

Deep Dive is optional depth. Your guided daily session remains the balanced path that keeps your overall knowledge portfolio broad.

DISCOVERY

See how your knowledge connects.

Discovery is a 2–3 minute curiosity break. Atlas takes something you already understand and connects it to another field through a shared mechanism, consequence, or system.

Discovery does not count as mastery. It is designed to spark curiosity and strengthen the network between subjects. If you want to learn the target deeply, turn it into a lesson.
